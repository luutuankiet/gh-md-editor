# v0.8.0 — steady images, quieter editor

- **GitHub-attachment images stop flickering on every keystroke** — a resolved attachment image (and the no-userscript fallback link) now survives the per-keystroke preview re-render instead of re-fetching and flashing a loading spinner each time you type. Same morphdom key + skip-update preservation the mermaid blocks already had, now extended to the userscript-resolved `<img>` and its fallback shell.
- **Removed the eager cursor-word highlighting in the editor** — placing the cursor on or near a word no longer lights up every other occurrence (inline highlight + scrollbar ticks). It fired on every keystroke and was just noise. `Cmd/Ctrl+F` stays as the way to find and highlight words; its scrollbar match ticks are unchanged.
